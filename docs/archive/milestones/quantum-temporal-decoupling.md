**Known limitations:** The quantum covers one Hart on the blocking TLM route only; native and flat routes have no local offset. `Q` >= 2 is reduced accuracy: a time-derived MTIP crossing may be recognized up to `Q - 1 + t` ticks late, and after the first late recognition a run is compared with its parity run by the D9 conservation identity, not turn by turn. Exact lateness is defined only where the guest and `Q` determine every sync point, so a debug session asserts the bound alone. The quantum is fixed for a run, with no adaptive, next-event-clipped, per-Hart or per-target quantum, and `tlm_quantumkeeper` is not used. The grant loop is the only quantum keeper because the SystemC smoke hosts one initiator. Every turn still samples the interrupt lines, so no wall-clock gain is claimed and A10 stays native-only. Issue #207, a possible underflow of a grant's consumed time when a guest lowers `mtime` inside a grant, was found during drafting, is unreproduced and stays open outside this contract. The C ABI is append-only, so a host built against a previous header must be recompiled. The SystemC smoke covers `atomic_tohost_exit` and `fd_fma` on Linux x86-64 at the pinned SystemC commit.

## 1. Objective and inherited profile

Let the single Hart of the TLM route run ahead of committed Platform time by a bounded local offset, syncing at a selected quantum and at a fixed list of forced points, under one delay-accumulation path. With the quantum off, a run is exactly today's run. With it on, time-derived timer recognition may be late by a declared bound, every other observable keeps its parity value, and committed time never drifts.

The inherited profile is everything delivered through the completed direct-memory contract: the selectable native or TLM RAM route, `ZeroDelayRam`, the atomic extension, declared-maximum delay accounting, direct memory, the `capi` C ABI and the pinned SystemC 3.0.2 smoke, over the default RV64GC profile with M/S/U, Debug Specification 1.0 and zero-entry PMP plus Sv39.

The inherited Stage 3 performance-certification waiver continues. `tools/a10/public-v1.json`, its fixtures and its pinned argv stay unchanged, and A10 runs only on the native route. This contract makes no wall-clock claim: every turn still samples the interrupt lines, and the proof that a quantum defers Platform synchronization is sync counts, not timing.

Vocabulary used throughout, in Platform ticks:

- `Q` is the selected quantum. `C` is committed time, the CLINT `mtime`. `O` is the local offset. `L` is the Hart's local time, `C + O` with wrapping addition.
- A *sync* is a boundary operation: it sets `C` to `L` and `O` to zero, then republishes `time` and the interrupt lines.
- A *flush* is the mid-turn F1 step: under the bus lock it adds `O` to `C` and sets `O` to zero. It is not a boundary, republishes nothing, is not counted and leaves `E` unchanged.
- `E`, the *anchor*, starts as `C` at install. Every sync sets it to `C`, and so does every other write to `C` except a flush: an idle jump, a guest or debug store to the CLINT `mtime`, the in-crate `set_platform_mtime`, and the direct advance of `advance_mtime` or `advance_mtime_by` at `Q` = 0, where `E` therefore always equals `C`.
- `t` is `turn_completion_bound_ticks()` of the selected route: a completed turn advances time by at most `1 + t`. A failed turn has no such bound, because an overshoot is what fails it.
- A *parity run* is the same run with `Q` = 0.
- A *time-derived crossing* is MTIP rising only because `mtime` reached `mtimecmp`, with no store to the CLINT in that turn.
- The *parity set* is `PARITY_GUESTS` in `scripts/run_elf_tests.sh`: `hello`, `s5_timer`, `s5_external`, `atomic_tohost_exit`, `fd_fma`, `direct_mem_smc_fence_i` and `direct_mem_sv39_pt_store`.

## 3. Forced syncs and ownership

The Machine owns the `quantum` selection as composition configuration, and the Platform owns `O` with the rest of Platform time, as amended in ADR-0003. The Hart is unchanged: it reads `time` and the lines exactly as it does now and never sees a quantum. In the SystemC host the external kernel still owns global time and commits only a grant's consumed amount.

A turn-end sync below runs after that turn's `advance` has entered `O`, before the boundary returns, on the path `Installed::turn` already uses to publish time. The `C` and `O` update takes only the bus lock, so it is callable from `&self`; the Hart CSR republish needs `&mut Installed`. Each row names where that matters.

An unmapped address is not a device. `DebugInbound` requests are F4's. Quiesce and drain | `request_quiesce` reaching a boundary, `OwnedMachine::quiesce` and `acknowledge_drain`. | Commit `O` inside `acknowledge_drain`, before `Installed::sample`. `quiesce` takes `&self` and `acknowledge_drain` holds `Option<&Installed>`, so this is the bus-lock update alone: it moves `C`, zeroes `O`, re-anchors `E` and counts under D3, and omits only the Hart CSR republish. `time` already holds `L` from the turn-end publish, and any later turn re-samples the lines at its start. | F9. Reset | `OwnedMachine::install`, `fresh_reset`, `reload_for_ndmreset` and `ruscv_machine_reset`, including the unknown-completion path that builds a fresh `OwnedMachine`. | No sync runs here. The old generation is already at `O` = 0 by F4, F6, F7 or F8. The fresh Platform starts with `C` = 0, `O` = 0 and `E` = 0. No offset crosses a generation. |
| F10. `u64` wrap | A zero-delay turn leaving `L < C`, which only an `mtime` near `u64::MAX` can produce, written by a guest or, before U2, by the in-crate `set_platform_mtime`. | Turn-end sync, so the wrapped value presents as it does in a parity run and MTIP is never stale high. |

Not sync points: RAM accesses, including atomics and direct-memory copies; reads of `time`, `mip` or `sip`; trap entry; MRET and SRET; writes to `mie`, `mstatus` or `sie`; FENCE, FENCE.I and SFENCE.VMA; a direct-memory invalidation, under the 2026-11-05 ADR-0004 amendment; commit-log observation, which carries no time; a zero `tohost` poll; and the Runner's typed reads through `Platform::tohost_value` and `Platform::signature`. D8 covers their effect on recognition. No public caller other than D11's request field and the D10 report can observe `O` non-zero, because every public stopping point above is a sync.

Required negative controls fall into three groups.

Other mutation-runner controls, with unit names prefixed `quantum-u<n>-`:

- `MachineConfig::new` selecting a non-zero quantum, or `Q` = 0 taking any path that changes `mtime`, events or a commit log;
- a non-zero quantum accepted on `Transport::Native`, `--quantum` accepted without `--transport tlm`, or the D2 overflow check applied to `Q` = 0;
- the threshold ignored, or tested so that a turn starts with `O >= Q`;
- a transport delay or direct-memory latency added twice, or dropped, between `advance` and `O`;
- `O` or a sync charged into `mcycle`, `minstret` or `transport_delay_femtoseconds`;
- debug work adding to `O`;
- a sync that loses or gains ticks, so `mtime` drifts from the D9 identity;
- CSR `time` presented from `C` instead of `L`;
- the overflow split replaced by one rule, so a parity run near `u64::MAX` diverges;
- an interrupt recognized later than the D8 bound, or MTIP derived from `L` so that the bound test's expected lateness changes;
- a flush counted as a sync, or a coincident forced sync clearing `quantum_reached`;
- `reduced_accuracy` or `interrupt_latency_bound_ticks` misreported, or the `Quantum:` line printed when off;
- `local_offset` non-zero when off, included in the charged delay, or read from `O` so that a RAM transaction after a flush loses the flushed ticks;
- `E` not re-anchored after a debug store to `mtime`, or the F1 mark surviving into the next guest turn;
- `committed_end_fs` read before the grant-return sync;
- a grant continuing past a sync, or past an idle jump into the wake turn, with `Q` non-zero;
- `boundary_reached` 0 for a sync-ended grant.

SystemC fault-option controls are not mutation-runner cases, because that runner runs only `cargo test --offline --all-features` and D14 keeps `--all-features` free of a C++ toolchain. U5 owns them as options of the smoke binary in the style of `--stop-after-grants`, and `scripts/test_systemc_smoke.sh` runs each under `--quantum 1024` with its own failure assertion, next to the early-`sc_stop` control. `main.cpp` prints `exit_code` for every terminal state, so neither assertion tests for its absence:

- `--fault-keep-offset`: the initiator returns the full `delay` without subtracting the offset. The run must report `result state=failed`, and its diagnostic must name the exceeded declared maximum, which is zero for `RamTarget`.
- `--fault-decrease-delay`: `RamTarget` lowers `delay` below the offset it received. The run must report `result state=failed`, and its diagnostic must name an unknown completion.

Keep the mutation patches, commands and expected assertions under the existing mutation-runner pattern. The standard gate and the no-ledger rule are unchanged.

## 5. Exclusions and retained direction

No `nb_transport`, TLM phases beyond blocking, TLM-modeled CLINT/PLIC/UART/HTIF, SystemC-modeled interrupt lines, multi-Hart or DMA, per-Hart or per-target quantum, a quantum that changes during a run, next-event clipping of the quantum, adaptive quantum, rollback, `tlm_quantumkeeper` or `tlm_global_quantum`, a sync triggered by a guest read of `time` or `mip`, block execution or translated code, removal of the per-turn line sample, direct memory for any device, multiple RAM targets, inbound masters other than the existing DM, hardware triggers, non-Linux FFI targets, package or crate publication, A10 change, ACT4 selection change, wall-clock performance claim, or new ISA extension or profile.

Non-blocking transport requires a separately approved timing contract. Stage 8 remains conditional.

## 6. Activation boundary

The rotation that activated this contract archived the direct-memory contract, applied the bounded 2026-11-06 quantum amendments to ADR-0002, ADR-0003 and ADR-0004, and asserted no runtime support.
