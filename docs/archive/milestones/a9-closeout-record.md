## Remaining limits, not successor obligations

- Unknown/poisoned domains quarantine resources until process exit. There is **no adapter termination/state-resolution API**; observing a late effect or joining its test thread does not authorize reset/reuse/drain or fabricate retrospective retirement/trap/exit.
- Admitted host writes remain competing real writers. Arrival can be nondeterministic; raw handles are volatile and external guards can block work. Flat signal polling/clearing use separate guards, not atomic read-and-clear or deterministic replay.
- Native devices and artifact suppression differ intentionally from flat offsets and artifact errors. Borrowed infallible `state_mut()` panics when it cannot prove drain. Typed/core-only helpers remain unmanaged compatibility routes; process fault injection is Unix-only.
- Fixed 32-bit public fetch and inherited profile limits remain. No full ADR, ISA, RV64A or VP certification; no new ACT4 run, MMU/PMP/page-walk integration, interrupts/WFI/time scheduler, debug/GDB, TLM/SystemC/DMI, multi-Hart/DMA/coherence, hardware timing, block/JIT, checkpoint, performance result or runtime optimization.
